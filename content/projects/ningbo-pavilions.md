## 1. The build

A multi-step archival fabrication: excavation, resin application, resin cure, depowdering, powder extraction, black primer, and a final resin seal. Each step had to be documented as carefully as it was executed — an archival piece outlives everyone who worked on it.

## 2. Why it matters for PM

Archival-grade work demands process documentation as rigorous as the physical craft — the same discipline behind a well-written PRD.
