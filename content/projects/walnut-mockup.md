## 1. The build

A four-step pipeline: glue-up layout → CNC layout → glue-up → sanding. Walnut was chosen for grain stability and finish density. The piece serves as a feasibility study for a larger archival production model.

## 2. Why it matters for PM

An MVP for a model. Build the smallest version that proves the method before committing to the full deliverable.
