## 1. The build

Sketch → drawings → scale model → A-frame fabrication → on-site transport → install. Section drawings document eight trusses with multiple fold options each — the geometry that lets flat timber become curved enclosure.

## 2. Why it matters for PM

Every decision on a drawing has a torque-wrench equivalent in the field. The project trained me to think about manufacturability and constraint long before I called it product management.
